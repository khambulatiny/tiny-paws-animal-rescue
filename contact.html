FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Adoption

How do I adopt an animal?

Complete the adoption enquiry form on our Adopt page. We will contact you to arrange a visit, do a quick home check and then finalise the adoption.

Is there an adoption fee?

Yes, a small fee helps cover vaccinations, sterilisation and vet checks. Please contact us for the current fee.

Can I adopt if I rent my home?

Yes, as long as your landlord allows pets. We may ask for written permission.

Volunteering

Do I need experience to volunteer?

No. We will show you everything you need to know. All you need is a love for animals and some free time.

How old do I need to be?

Volunteers must be 16 or older. Younger helpers are welcome when accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Donations

What are donations used for?

Donations pay for food, shelter, vaccinations, sterilisation and medical care for rescued animals.

Can I donate food or supplies instead of money?

Absolutely. Pet food, blankets, bowls, toys and leashes can be dropped off at 24 Market Street during opening hours.

Caring for rescued animals

How long does it take a rescued pet to settle in?

Every animal is different. Most need a few days to a few weeks. Give them a quiet space, a routine and lots of patience.

Can I contact you after adopting?

Yes! We are always happy to give advice after adoption, and we love receiving photo updates.
