Help us help them

Tiny Paws runs on the kindness of our community. Donate or volunteer and make a real difference.

Donate

What your donation pays for

Every rand goes directly to caring for rescued animals.

R100

Food

Feeds a rescued dog or cat for one week.

R250

Vaccinations

Covers core vaccinations for one animal.

R500

Vet care

Helps pay for sterilisation or medical treatment.

Items

Supplies

Blankets, bowls, toys, leashes and pet food are always welcome.

Ways to donate

  • EFT / bank transfer (details on the right)
  • Drop off food and supplies at 24 Market Street
  • Monthly debit order – ask us to set one up
  • Sponsor a specific animal until it is adopted

Join our volunteer team

No experience is needed – just a love for animals and a few hours to spare. Volunteers must be 16 or older (younger helpers are welcome with a parent).

  • Dog walking and exercise
  • Socialising and playing with cats
  • Cleaning kennels and feeding
  • Fostering animals at home
  • Helping at adoption days and fundraising events
